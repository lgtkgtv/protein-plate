# Contributing to ProteinPlate

Thanks for helping make healthy, affordable eating more accessible. You don't need to be a web developer — **all content is plain Markdown.**

## Ways to contribute

- 🥘 **Add or improve a recipe** (especially regional or vegetarian/vegan variants)
- ⚖️ **Refine portion or nutrition estimates** with better sources
- 🌍 **Translate** a page into another Indian language
- 🐛 **Fix typos, broken links, or unclear steps**

## How to make a change

1. **Fork** this repository and clone your fork.
2. Install the preview tool: `pip install mkdocs-material`
3. Run `mkdocs serve` and open `http://127.0.0.1:8000` — it live-reloads.
4. Edit the relevant file in `docs/` (e.g. `docs/recipes.md`).
5. Commit, push, and open a **Pull Request** with a short description.

## Style guide

- Keep instructions **short and numbered**; assume a busy home cook.
- Give quantities for a **family of 4**, and portions in **grams/ml** where possible.
- Prefer **verified video links** over copying others' text or photos.
- Use the existing tables and admonition styles for consistency.

## Content & health rules (please read)

- ProteinPlate is **educational, not medical advice**. Do not add claims that a
  food "cures", "treats", or "prevents" any disease.
- Cite reputable sources (e.g. ICMR-NIN, Harvard Nutrition Source, peer-reviewed
  data) for nutrition numbers when you can.
- Only contribute **your own photos** or openly licensed images; by submitting,
  you agree to license your contribution under **CC BY 4.0**.

## Licensing of contributions

By opening a pull request you agree that your **code/config** is contributed under
**MIT** and your **written content/photos** under **CC BY 4.0**.
