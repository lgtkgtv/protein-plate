# Grocery List

**Assumption (adjust freely):** this covers **one template meal per person per day for 7 days = 28 plate-servings**, rotating the protein anchor. If this is *both* lunch and dinner, roughly double the proteins, vegetables, and dairy.

**Suggested weekly protein rotation:** Paneer ×6 · Eggs ×6 · Chicken ×6 · Sprouts/moong ×5 · Chana ×5.

## Proteins & dairy

| Item                          | Approx. weekly quantity |
|-------------------------------|-------------------------|
| Paneer                        | 600–700 g               |
| Eggs                          | 2–2.5 dozen (24–30)     |
| Boneless chicken              | ~1.2 kg                 |
| Green moong (dry, to sprout)  | 500 g                   |
| Chickpeas / brown chana (dry) | 500 g                   |
| Curd / yogurt                 | ~2 kg                   |

## Vegetables

| Item                            | Approx. weekly quantity |
|---------------------------------|-------------------------|
| Broccoli                        | ~1 kg                   |
| Bell peppers (red/yellow/green) | ~1.5 kg                 |
| Mushrooms                       | ~500 g                  |
| Cabbage (green + purple)        | 1 each (~1.5 kg)        |
| Cucumber                        | ~2 kg                   |
| Tomato                          | ~2 kg                   |
| Onion                           | ~1.5 kg                 |
| Carrot                          | ~500 g                  |
| Spinach / methi                 | ~750 g–1 kg             |
| Lettuce                         | 2 heads                 |
| Green beans                     | ~500 g                  |
| Bottle gourd (lauki, for soup)  | 1                       |
| Avocado                         | 2–4                     |
| Lemon / lime                    | ~10                     |

**Aromatics & herbs:** Ginger ~150 g · Garlic 3–4 bulbs · Green chillies ~100 g · Coriander 3–4 bunches · Mint 1 bunch · Curry leaves.

## Nuts & seeds

| Item            | Weekly quantity |
|-----------------|-----------------|
| Almonds         | 250 g           |
| Walnuts         | 250 g           |
| Cashews         | 100 g (optional)|
| Pumpkin seeds   | 200 g           |
| Sunflower seeds | 150 g           |
| Flax seeds      | 100 g           |

## Pantry, flours, fats & spices

- **Flours:** Besan 500 g · Moong dal 500 g · Oats/daliya 500 g (optional) · a little jowar/bajra (optional).
- **Fats:** Groundnut / mustard / olive oil · Ghee 250 g.
- **For dips:** Tahini 1 small jar (or extra sesame seeds).
- **Spices:** turmeric, red chilli, Kashmiri chilli, cumin (seeds + powder), coriander powder, garam masala, chaat masala, tandoori masala, kasuri methi, amchur, hing, mustard seeds, ajwain, black pepper, bay leaf, salt.

!!! tip "Cost & waste"
    Dry moong and chana are the cheapest protein per gram here — sprouting at home costs almost nothing and triples the volume. Buy nuts/seeds in bulk monthly, not weekly.
